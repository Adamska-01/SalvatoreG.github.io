Upon joining [West Pier Studio](https://www.westpierstudio.com/), I was given the chance to take ownership of the development of a robust **vehicle customization system** in **Unreal Engine 5**. This was an attempt in trying to get visibility and acquire clients to co-develop AAA games.

As part of the process, I gained experience in:

- Developing a full vehicle customization system (Parts, colors, liveries).
- Implementation of **blueprints** that allow designers to easily create vehicle parts (Adding functionalities such as part/color/livery swap thanks to actor components) and add them to the game.
- Designed and implemented custom **UI widgets**, including a **carousel UI system**.
- Developed a custom **UI navigation** system that works with gamepads controls.
- Implemented a **custom camera view systems** for free-look, rotation, and UI-contextual focus views.
- **Collaborated with designers** to streamline workflows for vehicle asset imports and UI.
- Built a **portable custom Unreal Engine 5** from source with integrated **PS5 SDK tools**.
