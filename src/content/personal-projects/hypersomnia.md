**SleepyTime Studio** was created by a group of university students with the aim to create and publish a Horror game. We were no more than 9 individuals with a software development background.

As a **gameplay and asset programmer**, I was tasked with designing and implementing the following:

- Player stats (health, stamina, inventory, etc..).
- Adding functionalities to all interactable in the game.
- Blending the main character's animations.
- Pin Pad Puzzle mechanic.
- Async scene loader.
- Pause menu.
- I have also had the chance to make some 3D models using 3ds Max.

**Waterfall** methodologies were applied during development.
